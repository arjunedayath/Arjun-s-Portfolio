How Updates & Image Additions Work

Step-by-step workflow for updating your portfolio.

1. Instant Browser Sync: When you add or edit certificates, projects, or images here, changes are saved to Supabase. The public portfolio reads the cloud data and refreshes when updates arrive.

2. Image Uploads: You can upload certificate photos, project screenshots, or profile avatars directly from your computer. Images are automatically compressed and uploaded to your Supabase Storage bucket.

3. Export to HTML: Click "Download Ready HTML" anytime. This downloads the cloud-connected Arjun Aju Portfolio.html. Keep it with the Supabase-connected configuration when deploying your portfolio.
